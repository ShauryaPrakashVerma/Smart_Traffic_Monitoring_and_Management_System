Help & Information

Frequently asked questions about the public traffic portal.

Traffic colors indicate the current traffic conditions on the road network.

Green — Free flowing
Yellow — Moderate traffic
Orange — Heavy traffic
Red — Standstill / severe congestion

Open the Plan Journey section from the sidebar. Enter your starting point and destination to view available routes and their current traffic conditions.

Open Raise Complaint from the sidebar. Select the type of issue, provide its location and description, and submit the complaint.

A complaint reference number will be generated after successful submission.

Traffic information is updated continuously using available live traffic and monitoring data.

After planning a journey, tap the star icon on your preferred route to save it. Saved routes appear in the Saved Routes section.
